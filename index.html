DAF Grant Sync

An internal accounting integration operated by Livelihood Impact Fund.

DAF Grant Sync records charitable grants made from Livelihood Impact Fund's donor-advised fund accounts into the Fund's QuickBooks Online accounting records. It is built and operated by Livelihood Impact Fund for its own bookkeeping, and is not offered to, sold to, or usable by anyone outside the organization.

What it does

  • Reads grant records — date, amount, recipient charity, and reference number — from the donor-advised fund platforms the Fund grants through.
  • Creates a matching expense transaction in the Fund's QuickBooks Online company.
  • Checks existing QuickBooks records before writing, so a grant is never recorded twice.

What it does not do

  • It does not move money or initiate any payment.
  • It does not read or store customer, employee, or payroll data.
  • It does not share data with any third party.

Connecting and disconnecting

The integration is connected to QuickBooks Online once, by an administrator of the Fund's QuickBooks company, through Intuit's standard authorization screen. It runs as a scheduled script thereafter and has no separate sign-in.

To disconnect: in QuickBooks Online, go to Settings → Apps → DAF Grant Sync → Disconnect. Access ends immediately. No further data is read or written, and the Fund deletes the stored authorization tokens.
