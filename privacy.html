Privacy Policy

DAF Grant Sync · Livelihood Impact Fund · Effective 16 September 2026

DAF Grant Sync is an internal accounting integration operated by Livelihood Impact Fund ("the Fund"). It records the Fund's own charitable grants into the Fund's own QuickBooks Online company. It has no users outside the Fund and collects no information from the general public.

Information the integration accesses

When connected to QuickBooks Online, the integration accesses only what it needs to record grant transactions:

DataWhyRead or write
Chart of accountsTo identify which accounts grants are recorded againstRead
Expense transactions and their reference numbersTo check whether a grant has already been recordedRead
Expense transactionsTo record a grantWrite

It does not access customer records, employee or payroll data, bank credentials, payment card data, or any other part of the accounting file.

Separately, the integration reads grant records from the donor-advised fund platforms the Fund grants through. These contain the date, amount, recipient charity name, and a reference number for each grant. Recipient charities are organizations, not individuals.

Information the integration stores

  • Authorization tokens issued by Intuit, stored in a permission-restricted file on a Fund-controlled computer. These are the only Intuit-derived data retained at rest.
  • Grant records retrieved from donor-advised fund platforms, retained as part of the Fund's accounting records.

Accounting data read from QuickBooks is used during a run and not written to disk.

How information is used

Solely to record the Fund's grants in the Fund's own accounting system. The integration does not move money, initiate payments, or alter any record other than the expense transactions it creates.

Sharing

None. Data is not sold, rented, shared, or disclosed to any third party. There are no subprocessors, no analytics services, no advertising, and no data transfers outside systems the Fund controls, except the direct connection to Intuit's API.

Security

  • All communication with Intuit uses HTTPS (TLS 1.2 or higher).
  • Authorization tokens are stored with owner-only file permissions on full-disk-encrypted, access-controlled machines.
  • Access is limited to the small number of Fund staff who operate the integration.
  • Credentials are never committed to source control.

Retention and deletion

Authorization tokens are retained until access is revoked, then deleted. Grant records are retained for the period required for the Fund's financial records. Accounting data read from QuickBooks is discarded at the end of each run.

Revoking access

A QuickBooks administrator can revoke access at any time in QuickBooks Online under Settings → Apps → DAF Grant Sync → Disconnect. Access ends immediately and the Fund deletes the stored tokens.
